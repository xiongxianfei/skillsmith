# Travel Planning SOP

The canonical human-facing procedure is [references/sop.md](references/sop.md). It covers input intake, G0–G6 decision gates, flight and hotel feasibility, local transport, experience shortlisting, itinerary optimization, booking authorization and predeparture audits.

Keep the operational rules and their updates in that single source. This entry point exists for people opening `SOP.md` directly.

For the executable agent behavior, read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). For the complete examples and templates, see [README.md](README.md).
