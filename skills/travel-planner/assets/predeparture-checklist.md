# Predeparture quality checklist

## Essential: trip-ready status requires all checks complete
- [ ] Passport and relevant visa/entry/re-entry permission checked with current official sources.
- [ ] Each operating carrier meets the traveler's specified aviation-safety rule.
- [ ] Confirmed ticket number or PNR, passenger names, flights and any hotels match booking.
- [ ] Actual local dates/times, airport codes, terminal, baggage and separate-ticket risks checked.
- [ ] Fare refund/change restrictions accepted and receipt saved.
- [ ] Arrival transfer operates at the actual hour; hotel room/rest access confirmed.
- [ ] Work/event commitments and adequate sleep/recovery buffers protected.

## Connectivity and ground operations
- [ ] SIM roaming/local plan activated; alternative Wi-Fi and offline maps prepared.
- [ ] Local spending/card acceptance verified; small cash backup.
- [ ] Emergency phone numbers, hotel address and important confirmations saved offline.
- [ ] Taxi, bus/rail/transfer services and pickup instructions checked.
- [ ] Attraction reservations/hours and weather contingency checked.
- [ ] Booking support contacts and disruption alternatives available.

## Recommended verification cadence (not an automatic reminder)
- At booking: confirm issuance, all passengers/legs, fare rules and payment receipt.
- 72–48h before: confirm immigration updates, flight status, closures and local logistics.
- 24h before: check check-in requirements and actual flight terminal.
- Travel day: flight status, traffic, transfer time, connectivity and payment backup.
