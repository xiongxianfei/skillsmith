# Eyeglass frame taxonomy

Frame **shape**, **construction**, **material** and **size** are independent attributes. Explain them separately; a frame can be a titanium full-rim navigator, for example.

## Shape families for everyday prescription glasses

| Style | Defining outline | Typical effect | Fit consideration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rectangular | Wide relative to height, straight edges | Neat, structured | Very low lens height can constrain lens choices |
| Soft square / rounded rectangle | Angular overall with soft corners | Versatile, understated | Avoid excess total width |
| D-frame | Straighter brow and rounded lower edge | Classic with definition | Thick acetate can appear visually heavy |
| Panto | Rounded bottom, flatter upper curve | Refined, relaxed, vintage-modern | Bridge design affects position |
| Round / oval | Mostly circular or elongated oval | Soft or intellectual | Size and placement matter more than stereotypes |
| Browline | Pronounced upper rim, lighter lower section | Brow emphasis, mature | Can compete with thick brows |
| Squared navigator | Broad upper line, squared aviator-derived lens | Structured, confident | Large height can raise minus-lens edge thickness |
| Teardrop aviator | Tapering lower lens, often double bridge | Retro and expressive | Large lenses can be less compact for minus Rx |
| Geometric | Hexagon/octagon and other polygons | Contemporary, distinctive | Corners and size can dominate face |
| Cat-eye / butterfly | Upswept/extended outer top edges | Expressive, dramatic | Often more fashion-specific, but not excluded |

## Construction

- **Full-rim:** Lens surrounded by frame. Common and useful for concealing minus lens edges.
- **Semi-rimless:** Part of lens exposed; depends on lens-grooving support and chosen materials/design.
- **Rimless:** Lens supported at bridge/temples; low visual presence, requires appropriately designed lens and careful handling.

## Materials

- **Acetate:** wide colour and translucent options; mass depends on thickness/design.
- **Titanium:** light, corrosion resistant, sometimes flexible depending on construction.
- **Stainless steel:** thin, strong and often adjustable with nose pads.
- **Other polymers:** may be lightweight/flexible; tradeoffs vary by product.
- **Combination frames:** blend acetate brow/eyewires with metal bridges and temples.

## Do not overgeneralise

A frame being labelled "oval", "navigator" or "square" is not a guarantee of flattering proportions. Compare lens width, height, bridge, rim thickness, top brow line, total frame width and where the pupils sit inside the lens area. The wearer's existing comfortable frame is a valuable baseline. Colour should be tested against their real hair, clothing, lighting and preferences.

In exploration mode, use shape examples without claiming they are exact retail models. In buying mode, source concrete commercial product information separately and cite actual model data.
